# Funding

Research Canvas is free for everyone and always will be. It is not a business
and has no revenue model. This page says where money would come from, and what
it may and may not be used for.

## The rule

**Users never pay.** Not students, not researchers, not artists, not people
using it inside a company. There is no subscription, no paid tier, no "pro"
edition, and no feature withheld from the free version to sell later. If a
funding source would require breaking that, the project declines the money.

## Where support comes from

- **Grants** — research, arts, culture, digital-infrastructure and open-source
  funds. The natural fit, given who the tool is for.
- **Donations and sponsorship** — individuals and institutions that want the
  tool to keep existing.
- **Contribution in kind** — code, design, documentation, translation, testing.
  This is the most valuable form of support the project receives, and the only
  one it currently depends on.

## What money would be spent on

Infrastructure, an Apple Developer account so releases install without a
security warning, design and accessibility work, and paying contributors for
substantial work where possible. Spending is public.

## How it would be held

Through a neutral **fiscal host** (such as Open Collective) rather than a
personal account, so the ledger is public and nothing depends on one
individual's goodwill. Until a fiscal host is set up, the project takes no
money at all.
