# Code of Conduct

Research Canvas follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a friendly, harassment-free experience
for everyone — regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps our community:

- Being kind, patient, and welcoming, especially to newcomers
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments; personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove, edit, or reject
contributions, comments, or contributors that violate this Code of Conduct.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately by opening a confidential
issue or messaging [@saikiran9185](https://github.com/saikiran9185). Reports will be
handled discreetly and fairly.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (repo, issues, PRs, discussions) and when an
individual is representing the project in public spaces.
